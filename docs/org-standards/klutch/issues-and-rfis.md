# Klutch Issues and RFIs

## Issues are internal

PER ISSUES capture the full internal picture: background, evidence, options, Klutch's lean, and what is blocked. They can be as detailed as needed. Drafting to-dos are not issues; they belong in a punch or task list.

## RFIs are external

An RFI goes to people outside Klutch. It either asks them a question or **notifies them of an issue**, even when the answer is in Klutch's court. Reasons to notify include:

- it could affect the schedule;
- it will need coordination with others later;
- a site visit is involved that the client may want to attend;
- the client may be able to get the information simply by knowing it is needed.

RFI form:

- one question or one notification per RFI;
- written as a professional question or proposal to the reader, with the background the reader needs and nothing internal;
- references the Issue it came from (internal reference only).

## Issue to RFI needs owner approval

An Issue becomes an RFI only with the Klutch PE's approval. The working pattern: the agent and the PE review open Issues together, and the PE marks which ones become RFIs ("we don't know the answer, so generate an RFI on this issue"). The agent does not raise RFIs on its own.

## Validation

- Every RFI has a source Issue and the PE's approval recorded.
- Every RFI is addressed to someone outside Klutch, asks or notifies one thing, and contains no internal-only content.
- RFIs that are only internal questions are moved back to Issues.
