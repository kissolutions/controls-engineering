## Course user experience

1. **01 Academic Learning** — expanded chapters and foundational lessons.
2. **02 Engineering Playbooks** — step-by-step, clickable horizontal process navigator with individual lesson sections.
3. **03 Engineering Calculators** — reusable technical calculations, assumptions and interactive exploration.
4. **04 Knowledge Checks** — per chapter or engineering domain with feedback.

## Governance

- Never replace the frozen baseline with a working copy.
- Build subsequent work in separate engine/course directories, using versioned course packages.
- Keep canonical technical references and equations in the appropriate framework repositories.
- Training artifacts are derived instructional material and do not supersede engineering procedures, accepted project records or equipment manufacturer specifications.
