Polling & fault budgets

Protocol addressing, electrical unit loads, and the gateway's supported routes are separate constraints. Polling throughput is a fourth constraint: can the network meet its required scan interval?

A healthy FC03/FC04 transaction

For a read of R 16-bit registers, a normal RTU request is 8 bytes and a normal response is 5 + 2R bytes. Approximate serial wire time:

wire_time = (13 + 2 × R) × bits_per_character / baud

Then add measured/estimated device response, required silent gaps, and any gateway turnaround not already counted.

Worked example · 30 hypothetical sensors

Each device returns 10 registers using FC03 at 19,200 baud, 8E1 (11 bits/character), 20 ms device turnaround, 5 ms additional allowance, and a five-second target scan.

Nominal single transaction43.9 ms18.9 ms on wire + 25 ms allowance
Thirty-sensor scan1.32 s26.3% of the five-second budget

What if sensors stop responding?

At 500 ms timeout and two additional retries, an offline device may consume about 1.5 seconds instead of 43.9 ms. Three such failures can push the estimated full cycle to approximately 5.69 seconds—past the target.

Healthy utilization below 100% is not a guarantee of acceptable performance. Other TCP clients, queueing, failure retries and vendor timing rules can consume the remaining budget.
