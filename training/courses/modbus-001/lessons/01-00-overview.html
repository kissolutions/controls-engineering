Course overview

Design, commission, and troubleshoot a Modbus RTU sensor network behind a Modbus TCP gateway. The practical case is a synthetic 30-sensor vibration network; device limits are never assumed from the example.

Learn → Apply the playbook → Calculate → Demonstrate understanding. Experienced engineers can jump directly to a playbook, calculator, or knowledge check.
01 · Understand

RTU and TCP roles, RS-485, registers, routing, polling.

02 · Apply

Follow the engineering design and troubleshooting procedures.

03 · Calculate

Estimate normal and degraded serial-port performance.

04 · Prove

Answer domain checks and explain your diagnostic decisions.

Technical authority remains in the linked KIS Knowledgebase references and Controls Engineering playbooks. This training package adds instructional explanations and simulations, not new design standards.
